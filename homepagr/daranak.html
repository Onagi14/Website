About Daranak Falls

Daranak Falls is a beautiful waterfall located in Tanay, Rizal. Known for its cool, clear waters and stunning natural beauty, it's a popular destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers alike.

The falls cascade into a large pool surrounded by lush greenery, making it a perfect spot for swimming and relaxation.

Things to Do at Daranak Falls

  • Swimming in the natural pool
  • Picnicking by the falls
  • Hiking around the area
  • Photography of the stunning landscape
