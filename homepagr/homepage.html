Wawa Dam

Wawa Dam, located in Rodriguez (formerly Montalban), Rizal, is a popular spot for both locals and visitors thanks to its natural beauty and outdoor activities. The dam is nestled at the foot of the Sierra Madre mountain range, surrounded by lush greenery and rugged landscapes, making it an ideal destination for hiking, picnicking, and swimming in the reservoir. It's also a great spot for photography with its stunning views of the water and mountains.

Daranak Falls

Daranak Falls is one of the most scenic and popular natural attractions in Rizal, located in the nearby town of Tanay, but it’s easily accessible from Rodriguez (Montalban). The waterfall is known for its cool, clear waters that cascade into a large, natural pool at the base, perfect for swimming or just relaxing by the water. Surrounded by lush greenery, the falls offer a peaceful, refreshing escape into nature.
